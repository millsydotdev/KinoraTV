## Priority Legend

- **HIGH (Hard Gate):** Accessibility, contrast, focus visibility, broken theme tokens, banned AI indigo/purple accents, or viewport regressions.
- **MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate):** Over-applied glassmorphism, looping ambient animations against `MOTION 1`, colored left-stripe cards, excessive glow shadows, hardcoded theme-breaking hexes, or sub-44px interactive controls.
- **LOW (Quality Locks & Code Hygiene):** Copywriting buzzwords, wide-tracked uppercase labels, and decorative banner comments.
